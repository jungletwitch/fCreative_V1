About Us

Founded by Luigi Festinante, the studio merges European design discipline with advanced fabrication expertise.

A graduate of the European Institute of Design in Milan, Luigi later led the institute’s Phototyping and Modeling Laboratory, developing deep specialization in sculptural design, model making, and material innovation.

He went on to establish Art Design Lab, a studio rooted in the fusion of artistic vision and technical craftsmanship.

After more than a decade of international work, Luigi relocated to New York City to launch Fucking Creative — a studio driven by bold thinking, unconventional solutions, and the transformation of ambitious concepts into physical reality.

We create what others only imagine.
